New Service · Cannabis Facilities

Cannabis facility architecture & permitting in Los Angeles.

The City of Los Angeles Department of Cannabis Regulation (DCR) now ties license renewal to building-permit milestones. Every licensed cannabis business must reach a DCR Operating Permit by January 1, 2031. Alimo Architects is a licensed California architecture firm. We help dispensaries, delivery operators, cultivators, manufacturers and distributors assess their existing premises, prepare LADBS-ready plans, and move through each renewal cycle on time.

2027 DCR Renewal Period
September 1 – November 2, 2026

On the LIC-4025-FORM attestation, each applicant and licensee must show one of the following for the premises tied to its DCR Record Number:

A licensed California architect or engineer retained to prepare the building plans (name and license #)

or

An LADBS building permit application number for plans already submitted to plan check.

How We Help

From your LIC-4025 attestation to a Certificate of Occupancy.

  1. 01

    Architect of record for your renewal

    Once you retain us, we give you the architect's name and California license number for your LIC-4025-FORM. We also provide a retention letter for your records.

  2. 02

    Existing facility assessment

    We survey the site and document as-built conditions (including Scan to BIM). We review permit history, zoning and change of use, then flag work that could affect LADBS approval.

  3. 03

    Architectural plans & LADBS plan check

    We prepare building plans and tenant improvement drawings for your dispensary, cultivation, manufacturing or distribution space. We submit them with your DCR Referral for LADBS Plan Check (INT-4001-FORM) and answer plan check corrections.

  4. 04

    Accessibility & building-code compliance

    We design to California Building Code requirements, including CBC Chapter 11B accessibility: accessible routes, restrooms, counters and parking. We also address occupancy, exiting, fire and life-safety and security layout.

  5. 05

    Engineering & agency coordination

    We coordinate the structural, MEP and fire engineers. We track every clearance on your LADBS Clearance Summary Worksheet (CSW), including LAFD, LA Sanitation, LADWP, Bureau of Engineering and LA County Public Health.

DCR Renewal Milestones

The road to a DCR Operating Permit.

These milestones come from DCR Regulation No. 5(A)(8–9). LADBS says its first review of plans can take 6–12 weeks, so starting early matters.

  1. 2027 Renewal · Fall 2026

    Retain an architect or engineer

    Or get an LADBS building permit application number.

  2. 2028 Renewal · Fall 2027

    Plans submitted + CSW

    Have a building permit application number and a Clearance Summary Worksheet from LADBS.

  3. 2029 Renewal · Fall 2028

    Building permit + Public Health Permit

    Have an LADBS building permit and an LA County Public Health Permit for each activity.

  4. 2030 Renewal · Fall 2029

    Certificate of Occupancy

    Or a passed LADBS final inspection, if LADBS doesn't require a C of O.

  5. By Jan 1, 2031

    DCR Operating Permit

    Pass the DCR Final Inspection. Businesses can't operate without an Operating Permit.

Questions

Cannabis permitting, answered.

Who has to meet the 2027 renewal requirement?

Every cannabis applicant and licensee renewing with the Los Angeles Department of Cannabis Regulation. The requirement applies to the business premises tied to its DCR Record Number, whether that's a storefront retailer, delivery, cultivation, manufacturing, distribution or microbusiness location.

What do I put on the LIC-4025-FORM?

For the 2027 renewal cycle, you give either (a) your LADBS building permit application number or (b) the name and license number of the California-licensed architect or engineer you've retained. The license must be active on the Department of Consumer Affairs (DCA) website. When you retain Alimo Architects, we provide both details for option (b).

What if I haven't hired an architect yet?

The form includes a one-time extension request for this milestone. DCR is clear that it doesn't extend any later deadline. Your plans still need to reach LADBS early enough to get a Clearance Summary Worksheet by September 1, 2027. Retaining an architect now keeps that timeline in reach.

Can you work with my existing building?

Yes. Most cannabis businesses already operate in existing commercial or industrial buildings. We start by documenting what's there. Then we design the tenant improvements needed for building, fire and accessibility code compliance and plan how to get them permitted and built.
